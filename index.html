  • Wipe down

2012 Gibson Les Paul Studio Satin

Guitar commissioned to be setup by Raye Smith from Raye Ann Pyrography (formerly Cinderbella Studios), the artist behind the beautiful art you see burned onto the guitar. We were blessed to be able to disassemble and sand the guitar for her work and reassemble it for a full setup. The guitar is now a beautiful work of art and plays like a dream. We are honored to have been a part of this project and to have had the opportunity to work with such a talented artist.
