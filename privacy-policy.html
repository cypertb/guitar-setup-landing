DB Guitar Setup (“we,” “our,” or “us”), a sole proprietor operating as DB Guitar Setup, values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you contact us, request a setup, or send a text message.

Text Messaging

By opting in to text messages, you confirm that you are the owner or authorized user of the phone number and agree to receive setup-related SMS communications from DB Guitar Setup. Message frequency varies. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. Message and data rates may apply.

Data Sharing

We may share information with trusted service providers only when needed to operate our business and communicate with you. We do not buy, sell, rent, or trade personal information or lead lists. We only use customer information to provide setup services and related communications.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is completely secure. Please do not send sensitive information via text message unless necessary.

Your Rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to opt out of communications.
